I’m a software engineer driven by curiosity for how systems work beneath the abstraction layers. Most of my career has been focused on building secure, scalable backend systems, but my involvement in open source pushed me far beyond traditional backend engineering into infrastructure, systems programming, distributed systems, and protocol-level thinking.

I strongly believe in the power of open-source collaboration and community-driven engineering. Through open source, I’ve had the opportunity to contribute to software and infrastructure relied upon by millions of users globally while learning from some incredibly talented engineers and communities.

Currently, my focus is shifting deeper into systems and protocol engineering — particularly around blockchain infrastructure, distributed coordination, AI agent systems, and the intersection between intelligent execution layers and decentralized technology. I’m especially interested in building reliable systems with Go and Rust that operate close to the infrastructure and protocol layer.

When I’m not at the terminal, I’m usually out practicing **photography** 📸, reading 📚, or deep-researching historical events, systems, and ideas that shape the modern world.
